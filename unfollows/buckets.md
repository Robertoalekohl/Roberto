# The 211, in three piles

The other conversation offered to split the names. The export itself isn’t in this thread — only the shape of it (211 / 935, plus the people named out loud). This is that split, using everything that *was* said.

If you later paste the 211, they can go into these same three columns name by name. Until then, don’t treat the famous ones and the human ones as one event.

---

## A. Totally unsurprising

Not a social decision about you. Reciprocity was never on the table.

**Named:** Arca · Charli XCX · Kelela · FKA twigs · Oklou

**Also this pile by type:**

- other artists at that scale
- big NYC venues
- publications / institutions
- brands and shops that aren’t actually in a two-way relationship with you
- anyone you follow as culture, not as a person who knows your name

**What it means:** nothing about last year’s conversations. These names only hurt because they were printed in the same list as people who *do* know you.

---

## B. Probably meaningless — housekeeping, algorithm, feed hygiene

The default for most of the 211.

- following-count diets
- periodic purges (50–100 at a time; you either survived that night or you didn’t)
- “I don’t follow people I don’t really know”
- professional vs personal feed split
- they changed what Instagram is *for*
- relationship / life-admin change
- old follow, account unrecognized
- accidental unfollow
- followed because a connection was briefly useful, then the connection went idle
- they like you and still don’t want the posts in the stream

**What it means:** you were not a scene. You were a row. That’s rude as a technology. It is not a review of your work or of whether you “add pollution.”

---

## C. Actually weird / the ones that hurt

Not because the math is different. Because **you have a memory attached**.

- people you interacted with in the last year
- people you talked with about potentially doing something
- NYC artists, DJs, photographers, nightlife, creative acquaintances who felt like they were *in* something with you
- anyone where your honest reaction is *wait, YOU unfollowed ME?*

**What it means:**

- the interaction can still have been real
- the relationship often never consolidated into a feed
- sometimes it *is* a quiet social trim (“I don’t want this person in my digital circle”)
- it is still not reliable evidence that they secretly disliked you, found you boring, or decided you were pollution
- you will not get a satisfying explanation from the app

This is the only pile that deserves the *what the fuck*.  
It is also the pile with the worst signal-to-noise. One real snub and ten housekeeping casualties feel identical when you’re staring at usernames.

---

## How to use this without the full export

When a name from the list floats up:

1. If you would never expect them to follow you back → **A**. Let it go.
2. If you don’t have a specific memory from this year → **B**. Default.
3. If you can picture a conversation → **C**. Feel it. Don’t promote it to a theory of your worth. Don’t hunt their profile for a reason. There usually isn’t one you can use.

Follow people because you want to see them, not because the list should have been symmetrical.
